WHO WE ARE
Building the Next Generation of Builders.
eSTEAMED Academy is a Sacramento-region STEAM education company serving youth ages 5–19. We run hands-on enrichment classes, competitive robotics and esports leagues, and school-based expanded learning programs — all designed around one idea: kids learn best when curiosity has somewhere to go.
We believe STEM shouldn&apos;t feel like homework. It should feel like a team you make the cut for, a build you can&apos;t wait to finish, and a season you&apos;re counting down to. That&apos;s why everything we do is built on the same principle that makes youth sports so powerful — practice, teams, seasons, and a place to show what you&apos;ve built.

WHAT WE DO
Three Programs. One Mission.
eSTEAMED Academy operates three connected programs, each designed to meet kids and families where they are:
eSTEAMED Academy &amp; Makerspace
Our in-person home base. Weekly enrichment classes in coding, game development, 3D printing, and AI, plus open Makers Mondays sessions where kids can build on their own projects using our tools and space.
eSTEAMED Labs &amp; Leagues
Our competitive track. Seasonal robotics and esports teams — including VEX IQ robotics and our proprietary eSTEAMED STEAM Challenge League — that turn STEM skills into team sport. Kids practice, compete, and climb toward championship season, just like they would on a soccer or basketball team.
eSTEAMED Expanded Learning Academy
Our school partnership arm. We bring STEM enrichment, youth development, and esports programming directly into afterschool programs (ELOP) at partner districts across the Sacramento region.

OUR WHY
Where Curiosity Meets Competition.
Our mission is simple: make STEM participation as common — and as exciting — as youth soccer. Every class, team, and league we run is built to turn curiosity into confidence, and confidence into competition-ready skill.
Whether your child is 3D printing their first design, coding their first game, or gearing up for a robotics championship, eSTEAMED Academy is where they build it.
