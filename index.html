PROGRAM // ACADEMY & MAKER SPACE

eSTEAMED Academy
& Maker Space

Come in. Build something. Take it home.

Our makerspace at 4625 44th Street is where ideas become real things. Every class is designed around making — not just watching. Students use professional equipment, learn real skills, and walk out with something they built themselves.

Classes Offered

Game Development

AGES 10–18 · BEGINNER–INTERMEDIATE

Students design and build their own video games using industry-standard tools — game mechanics, level design, character creation, and basic programming logic.

They'll make: A playable game they designed and built — ready to share.

3D Printing Class

AGES 8–18

Introduction to 3D modeling and printing. Students learn beginner-friendly CAD software and watch their designs come to life on our 3D printers.

They'll make: A custom 3D-printed object — designed by the student, printed in class.

Build & Take Home Your Own 3D Printer

AGES 12–18 · ADVANCED / MULTI-SESSION

The deep-dive version. Students build a 3D printer from a kit — learning the mechanics, how to calibrate it, and how to troubleshoot common issues.

They take home: Their own assembled and calibrated 3D printer.

PROGRAM // LABS & LEAGUES

eSTEAMED
Labs & Leagues

Learn it in the fall. Compete in the spring.

Our seasonal STEAM competition program. In the fall, students build skills through structured labs. In the spring, they join competition teams and represent eSTEAMED on the regional stage.

FALL SEASON · 16 WEEKS

Fall — Labs (Classes)

Our fall program runs 16 weeks to give students a strong foundation before competition season. Labs cover the skills, teamwork, and strategy that drive competition success.

Battlebots Robotics Labs

Students design, build, and program combat robots using the VEX platform — learning engineering principles, strategy, and iterative design.

VEX GO · K–3 VEX IQ · 4–8 VEX 5 · 9–12

FIRST Lego League

Teams work on a research project and build and program a robot to complete missions on a themed field — developing collaboration, problem-solving, and presentation skills.

iRacing League

Introduces students to sim racing and motorsport technology — race strategy, vehicle setup basics, and sportsmanship in a competitive eSports environment.

Get STEAMED League

A cross-disciplinary lab combining science, technology, engineering, art, and math into team-based competitions — for students who want to compete but haven't found their specialty yet.

eSports Leagues

Structured competitive gaming with purpose. Our eSports leagues emphasize communication, strategy, and team-building alongside competitive play in organized league formats.

SPRING SEASON · STARTS MAY

Spring — Competition Teams

Starting in May, eSTEAMED launches official competition teams. Students who completed the fall lab season are eligible to join a team and compete in regional events.

MAY
Team formation begins
MAY–JUNE
Training, strategy & practice matches
LATE SPRING–SUMMER
Regional competition events
Team eligibility: Preference is given to students who completed the 16-week fall lab session. New students may be accepted based on availability — contact us to learn more.

// Company Overview

About eSTEAMED Academy, LLC

eSTEAMED Academy is dedicated to building the next generation of makers, engineers, coders, and innovators. We serve youth ages 5–19 through hands-on classes, competitive robotics programs, and expanded learning partnerships with school districts across the Capital Region.

From our makerspace at 4625 44th Street to after-school programs in classrooms across Sacramento, eSTEAMED brings real-world STEAM skills to young people who are ready to build something that matters.

eSTEAMED is always looking for passionate educators and youth workers who want to do more than lead a class — we want people who want to build something with us. We hire for impact. If you love working with young people, know your craft, and believe hands-on learning changes lives, we want to hear from you.

Open Roles

● EVERGREEN · ALWAYS ACCEPTING

STEAM Instructor

PART-TIME / CONTRACT · IN-PERSON · SACRAMENTO, CA

eSTEAMED Instructors deliver hands-on STEAM classes and makerspace sessions for youth ages 5–19. You'll teach subjects from game development and 3D printing to robotics and coding, depending on your skills and our scheduling needs.

WE'RE LOOKING FOR
• Experience working with youth (ages 5–19)
• Skills in coding, game dev, 3D printing/design, robotics, or electronics
• Ability to manage a classroom and keep students engaged
• Flexibility to work across makerspace and school-site locations
• Team-oriented, reliable, and ready to grow with us

Site Coordinator

PART-TIME / CONTRACT · IN-PERSON · SACRAMENTO, CA

eSTEAMED Site Coordinators are the on-the-ground leads for our school-based expanded learning programs. You'll handle daily operations at your assigned site — managing attendance, supporting instructors, communicating with school staff, and ensuring a safe, productive experience.

WE'RE LOOKING FOR
• Prior experience in a youth program, school, or community org
• Strong communication and organizational skills
• Comfortable managing logistics and people simultaneously
• Background check required — must meet school site clearance
• Bilingual (English/Spanish) a plus
